**AI AND TECH**

- Oct 27, 6:00 to 8:30 PM: Cursor Meetup Philadelphia (October), Indy Hall, Philadelphia. [link](https://luma.com/cursor-c2mq). Monthly meetup for Cursor and AI coding tool users with lightning talks; free, RSVP required on Luma.

- Nov 5, 6:30 to 8:30 PM: Greater Philly AI: Show and Tell #1, Phoenixville Regional Chamber of Commerce, Phoenixville. [link](https://www.meetup.com/greater-philly-ai/). New informal AI builder meetup just 15 minutes from Audubon, bring a laptop and show something you built; free.

**CERAMICS AND CLAY**

- Oct 24 to 25, 10 AM to 5 PM: PA Guild of Craftsmen Fall Fine Craft Fair, Greater Philadelphia Expo Center, Oaks. [link](https://craftcouncil.org/?p=24494). Juried fine craft fair with a real ceramics contingent, 5 minutes from home; $10 admission, free parking.

- Nov 5, 4:00 to 9:00 PM: Philadelphia Museum of Art Craft Show Preview Gala, PA Convention Center, Philadelphia. [link](https://pmacraftshow.org/). First access to 195 juried craft artists including ceramicist Roberto Lugo and juror Lisa Naples; preview gala tickets start at $250, pricey (the cheaper public days with a ceramics speaker panel start Nov 6, just after this window).

**DANCE**

- Thursdays, Oct 22, 29, and Nov 5, beginner lesson 7:30 PM, dancing 8:00 to 10:30 PM: Thursday Night Contra Dance, Commodore Barry Irish Center, Philadelphia (Mt. Airy). [link](https://thursdaycontra.com/). Live band, caller walks beginners through every dance, no partner needed; sliding scale, about $5 to $20.

- Thursdays, Oct 22, 29, and Nov 5, beginner class 7:00 to 8:00 PM, milonga follows: Beginner Tango Class and Milonga, Philadelphia Argentine Tango School, Philadelphia (Fishtown). [link](https://philadelphiatangoschool.com/beginner). Weekly beginner class feeding straight into a social dance, no partner needed; $10 drop-in.
