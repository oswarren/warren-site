**NEAR APPOMATTOX VA**

**TECH AND MAKER**
- Sept 4: Vector Space First Friday Open House, Lynchburg, VA. [link](https://vector-space.org/events) (free tour of the community makerspace: 3D printing, woodshop, electronics benches; good low key first visit)
- Sept 11: Lynchburg Amateur Radio Club meeting, Lynchburg, VA. [link](https://k4cq.com/) (open to the public, 7:30 PM at the Fire Training Center; second Friday club night)

**OUTDOORS AND ROCKHOUNDING**
- Sept 4: History Hike, James River State Park, near Gladstone VA. [link](https://www.dcr.virginia.gov/state-parks/event?id=2026-03-03-21-34-12-627629-40c) (ranger led 2 mile hike covering fossils and Civil War history, ends at the Tye River overlook)

**MUSIC AND ARTS**
- Sept 7: Joel Sweeney and the Banjo Festival, Appomattox Court House NHP. [link](https://www.nps.gov/apco/planyourvisit/special-events.htm) (headliner Dom Flemons, plus dedication of a new historical marker to Black banjoists)
- Sept 8: Celtic Thunder, Academy Center of the Arts, Lynchburg, VA. [link](https://academycenter.org/event/celtic-thunder/) (touring Irish vocal group, 7:30 PM show)
- Sept 8: Charlottesville Chamber Music Festival opening concert, Charlottesville, VA. [link](https://www.cvillechambermusic.org/festival-2026.html) (worth the longer drive if you want serious chamber music)
- Sept 10: Lynchburg Virginia Elvis Festival, Academy Center of the Arts, Lynchburg, VA. [link](https://academycenter.org/event/lynchburg-virginia-elvis-festival-2026/) (three days of tribute performances downtown)

**TECH AND MAKER**

**MUSIC AND ARTS**
- Sept 5: Goo Goo Dolls with Neon Trees, Pavilion at Montage Mountain, Scranton. [link](https://www.montagemountainamp.com/events/goo-goo-dolls-05-september-2026/) (a proper big name concert if you want one)

- Sept 11 to 13: Pittsburgh Irish Festival, Carrie Furnace, Pittsburgh. [link](https://pghirishfest.org/tickets/) (35th year: live music and dance at a striking industrial riverside site)
