---
title: Work with me
description: Say what you keep meaning to do, and see the system I would build around it.
aliases: [make-one]
---

Tell me something you keep meaning to do. What comes back is the system I would build around it: what it reads, what it decides, what arrives, and what your part is. It is a small demonstration of how I think, and it is yours whether or not you write to me.
