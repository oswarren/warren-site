---
title: Balance
description: What runs without me, and what still needs my hands.
---

Two lists. On the left, the things that happen whether or not I show up. On the right, the things that still need my hands.

The point of the page is the second list getting shorter. Not because that work stops mattering, but because anything that can run on its own should, so what is left is the part only I can do: the clay, the ground, the words, the people.

Underneath, every time something crossed from one side to the other.
