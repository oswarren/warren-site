Short version of a longer log: one small digital product, built end to end with an assistant doing most of the typing, listed on Gumroad, first sale on Jul 30. The tool that did it is now [retired](../tools); this note is the part worth keeping.

## what worked

Treating the assistant as a fast, forgetful pair. Every session started from a one-page brief and ended with a line in the log. Decisions lived in the brief, not in the chat.

## what did not

Polishing. See [the library-sale-watch report](home-cooked).
