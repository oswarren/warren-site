Most of what I build is small: an email that arrives on Thursday, a check that runs while I sleep. What surprises me is how much a small thing changes once it runs on its own, so I write each one up when it is done: what the problem was, what I built, what it changed. No schedule; a note goes out when a system has earned one.

Each post is listed below; open one to read it.
