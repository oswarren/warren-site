---
title: Night Shift
description: The system that keeps my projects finishing instead of restarting.
log: night-shift
---

I start a lot of projects, and I am good at the first eighty percent. The last mile is where I stall: the click that publishes something, the account I have to create, the phone call, the number I need to look up. Left alone, I restart instead of finishing, and a second version of the same idea appears next to the first.

Night Shift is the fix. Every project I run keeps a plain text note with where I left off and what comes next. Three times a day, a program (Claude Code, running in the cloud, with my computer off) reads every note, reads back what I ticked off or answered in my to-do list, records what got finished, and puts the day's five next steps in front of me: one longer sitting, one decision that is coming due, and three small steps, each written out so I can do it without opening anything else. If I let something sit for a few days it nudges; if I ignore it for three weeks it parks it and says so.

I answer in the to-do app with a word or two. Everything else, the bookkeeping, the log of finished things, the ordering, it does. The lines below are its runs: how many things it closed and how many steps it set out.
