When one of my systems has run long enough to trust, I package it: the routine or the files, a plain guide, and nothing that needs me afterward. Three are out so far.

- **Local Events Radar.** The routine behind Porch Light, set up for your own town and your own interests. [On Gumroad](https://opensourcewarren.gumroad.com/l/local-events-radar).
- **Personal Automation Kit.** A short guide and seven recipes for setting up routines that run on their own (morning briefs, watchers, digests, file cleanup), written for people who do not program. [On Gumroad](https://opensourcewarren.gumroad.com/l/personal-automation-kit).
- **Quartz 5 Themes.** Three looks for a Quartz site (this site runs on Quartz), plus a free one called Field Notes. [The pack](https://opensourcewarren.gumroad.com/l/quartz5-themes) and [Field Notes, free](https://opensourcewarren.gumroad.com/l/quartz5-field-notes).

A line appears below each time a kit ships.
