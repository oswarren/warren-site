## The curiosity

I built this after a year at a ceramics residency, when I realized I still had not met anyone outside the program. I can be a homebody. The problem was never a shortage of things happening; it was that finding them took exactly the effort I was not making. So I made the finding happen without me.

## What it does

Every Thursday evening it searches for real, in-person events in the next ten days, in three categories I chose (tech and maker, outdoors and rockhounding, music and arts), checks that each one has a date, a place, and a working link, keeps the best few, and emails me one short list I can read in under a minute. Then it posts the digest here.

## The system

A prompt, a schedule, and a mail connection, running in the cloud with my computer off. There is no server of mine and nothing to keep alive. The digest it sends is the record: every one it has ever sent is listed below, exactly as it went out.

## Cause and effect

Thursday, 6pm. Nothing from me. By 6:07 there is a list in my inbox and a copy on this page.

## What changed

I go to things. And the shape turned out to be useful to other people: a version tuned for a ceramics residency arrives every Thursday for its resident artists, and the routine itself is packaged so it can run for your own town.
